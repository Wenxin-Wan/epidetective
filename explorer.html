Suspected link217 — Limited evidence in humans

A credible association exists, but chance or bias cannot yet be excluded. Limited is not the same as harmless — it means the question is still open.

Before you draw conclusions

Three things this map does not tell you.

Hazard is not risk

That something can cause cancer does not mean it caused yours.

A Group 1 classification means an exposure is capable of causing cancer under some conditions. How much, for how long, at what age, and with which genes all sit between that statement and any individual person.

Limited is not safe

An absent link may mean nobody has looked hard enough yet.

The map records what has been studied and reviewed. Gaps are as often gaps in research funding and follow-up time as they are genuine absences of harm.

Not medical advice

This is a map of populations, not a verdict on a patient.

If you are living with a cancer diagnosis, the people treating you have information about your case that no population-level map contains. Bring questions to them.
