Before you draw conclusions

Three limits of this map, worth knowing before you read it.

Hazard and risk

Something that can cause cancer may still have had nothing to do with yours.

A Group 1 classification means an exposure can cause cancer under some conditions. How much, for how long, at what age, and which genes you carry all stand between that finding and any one person.

Gaps in the map

A missing link may only mean nobody has looked hard enough yet.

The map records what has been studied and reviewed. Many gaps come from limited research funding or short follow-up time, so an empty space here is weak evidence of safety.

Whole populations

This map describes populations, and cannot judge any single case.

If you are living with a cancer diagnosis, the people treating you know things about your case that no population map contains. Bring your questions to them.
