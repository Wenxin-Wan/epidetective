Code Against Cancer

Concrete, evidence-based things you can do to lower your own risk of cancer — drawn from the regional Codes Against Cancer developed by IARC (the WHO cancer agency) and its partners.

The World Code Against Cancer Framework

One evidence-review method, adapted region by region. Each regional Code is developed by IARC together with a regional coordinating partner, then tailored to local cancer patterns and living conditions. Europe and Latin America & the Caribbean are published; Asia is in development.

How to use this. This page summarises official guidance for your information. It is not medical advice. Screening ages, vaccination schedules and workplace rules vary by country — follow the programme available where you live. For anything concerning your own health, speak to a doctor.
