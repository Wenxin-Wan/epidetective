First, the four labels behind every entry ↓

Group 1

Known cause

The agent can cause cancer in people. This is the group in which IARC is most certain.

It does not say how large the risk is, or at what dose. Processed meat and tobacco smoking are both in Group 1, with very different risks.

See how different →

Group 2A

Probable cause

The agent probably causes cancer in people. The evidence falls short of Group 1, most often because the studies in people are limited.

It does not say that it causes cancer at the levels people usually meet, or how large any risk is.

Group 2B

Possible cause

The agent may cause cancer in people. One kind of evidence points that way.

It does not say that it probably causes cancer, or that people exposed to it are at risk.

Aspartame is in this group →

Group 3

Not classifiable

The evidence does not meet the rules for any other group, usually because there is too little of it.

It does not say that it is safe. Group 3 describes the state of the evidence.
