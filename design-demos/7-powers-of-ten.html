1 person

No study can tell you what caused one person’s illness.

10 people

Still far too few to see a pattern.

1,000 people

Now patterns appear. Among 1,000 smokers, about 100 to 120 get lung cancer by age 75. Among 1,000 lifelong non-smokers, 4 to 5.

Millions

The evidence pools whole populations over decades. That is the scale the tools describe, and the scale at which “how sure” can be answered.
