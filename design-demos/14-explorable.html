One idea

Same certainty, very different size

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1: the evidence that each can cause cancer is equally sure. The risks are not. About 10 to 12 in 100 smokers get lung cancer by age 75, compared with 4 to 5 in 1,000 lifelong non-smokers.

per 100 vs per 1,000. Same label!
