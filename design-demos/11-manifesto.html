A hazardis nota risk.

A hazard says whether something can cause harm. Risk says how likely harm is, at the levels people meet. IARC’s groups rate hazard.

Sureis notbig.

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1: equally sure, very different risks. About 10 to 12 in 100 smokers get lung cancer by age 75.

A populationis nota person.

The tools describe patterns across large groups of people. They cannot tell you what caused any one person’s illness. Seven ideas for reading a health headline →
