Scroll for one idea that changes how you read a health headline

Picture 1,000 people.

Suppose all of them smoke. By age 75, about 100 to 120 of them will have had lung cancer.

Now suppose none of them ever smoked. About 4 to 5 will.

Tobacco smoking is in Group 1, the label for known causes. So is processed meat, which raises bowel-cancer risk by 18% for each 50 grams eaten a day. The label says how sure the evidence is. It does not say how big the risk is.

That is one of seven ideas that make a health headline easier to weigh.
