Same certainty, very different size

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1. The evidence that each can cause cancer is equally sure. About 10 to 12 in 100 smokers get lung cancer by age 75; each 50 grams of processed meat a day raises bowel-cancer risk by 18%.
