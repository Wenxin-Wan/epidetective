Iof III

A hazard is not a risk.

Group 1 means the agent can cause cancer in people. It is the group in which IARC is most certain. It does not say how large the risk is.

The four groups say how sure the evidence is, from known cause to not classifiable.

  1. 1Known causeThe agent can cause cancer in people.
  2. 2AProbable causeThe agent probably causes cancer in people.
  3. 2BPossible causeThe agent may cause cancer in people. Aspartame is in this group.
  4. 3Not classifiableThe evidence does not meet the rules for any other group. It does not mean safe.

IIof III

Sure is not big.

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1: the evidence that each can cause cancer is equally sure. The risks are not.

Same certainty, very different size.
