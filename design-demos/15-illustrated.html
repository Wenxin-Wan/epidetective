One idea

Same label, very different risk

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1, the label for known causes. The label tells you how sure the evidence is. It does not tell you how big the risk is.

10–12 in 100smokers get lung cancer by age 75
4–5 in 1,000lifelong non-smokers do
