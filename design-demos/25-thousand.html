Same certainty, very different size

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1: the evidence that each can cause cancer is equally sure. The risks are not.

Of 1,000 smokers, about 100 to 120 get lung cancer by age 75.

About 10 to 12 in every 100: one full block of the ten, and part of the next.

Of 1,000 lifelong non‑smokers, about 4 to 5 do.

Same label. Very different risk.

Seven ideas for reading a health headline →

The label says how sure the evidence is, not how large the risk is

  • Group 1Known cause

    The agent can cause cancer in people. This is the group in which IARC is most certain.

  • Group 2AProbable cause

    The agent probably causes cancer in people.

  • Group 2BPossible cause

    The agent may cause cancer in people. Aspartame is in this group.

  • Group 3Not classifiable

    The evidence does not meet the rules for any other group. It does not mean safe.
