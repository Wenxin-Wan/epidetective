Four questions,answered from the tools

Is aspartame dangerous?

In June 2023 IARC classified aspartame as possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B), a rating of hazard. WHO says a 70-kilogram adult would need more than 9 to 14 cans of diet drink a day to pass [the acceptable daily intake].

Quoted from the Cancer Evidence Explorer. Open the Aspartame entry →

Is processed meat as bad as smoking?

Same certainty (Group 1), very different size.

Each 50 grams eaten every day raises the risk of bowel cancer by 18%. In European data, about 10 to 12 in 100 smokers get lung cancer by age 75, compared with 4 to 5 in 1,000 lifelong non-smokers.

Quoted from the Cancer Evidence Explorer. Open the Processed meat entry → See also Relative and absolute risk.

Does my job raise my cancer risk?

68 workplace agents and jobs are on the cancer map, from asbestos and silica dust to night-shift work and firefighting.

Painters with over 10 years in the trade had about 1.8 times the bladder-cancer risk of non-painters. Lung-cancer risk rises with the total silica breathed over the years.

Quoted from the Cancer Evidence Explorer. Work and cancer, sector by sector →

What raises stroke risk?

Twelve risk factors are graded for ischaemic stroke, from blood pressure, smoking and diabetes (Strong) to depression (Suggestive).

High blood pressure is the leading cause of stroke.
