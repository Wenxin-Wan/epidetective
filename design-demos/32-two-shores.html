What causes cancer and other chronic diseases, and how sure the evidence is

Free tools built from the IARC Monographs and published reviews by Wenxin Wan, a cancer epidemiologist.

They describe whole populations, not what caused any one person’s illness.

1

Sorted by certainty

The left shore regroups by how sure IARC is: known causes at the top in red, then probable, then possible causes in grey.

2

Sorted by number of links

Now the exposures linked to the most cancer types rise to the top. Each line is one link; a dashed line is a suspected link.

3

Group 1 only

Known cause: the agent can cause cancer in people. This is the group in which IARC is most certain. It does not say how large the risk is.

4

Same certainty, very different size

Tobacco smoking and processed meat are both in Group 1: the evidence that each can cause cancer is equally sure. The risks are not.
