Help me decide

Set how much each quality matters to you. The list re-ranks from my scores for each demo (1 to 5, my judgement after building them).

    Scores are a starting point, not a verdict. Open your top two or three and scroll each one on a phone as well as a laptop.

    The ten directions

    My recommendation

    Direction 6, Thousand. It does what you asked most directly: the page tells one short story as you scroll, and the story is your site’s main lesson. A field of 1,000 people changes step by step, from smokers to non-smokers to “same label, very different risk”, then the tools arrive. Dots read the same in every language, it stays light and calm for the inner pages, and it is the scrollytelling pattern journalists and clinicians already trust.

    If you want it to feel more personal, Direction 14, Explorable, is the strongest alternative and is the best fit for the long reading pages. For the most memorable front page, Direction 7, Powers of Ten, makes the “populations, not persons” point unforgettable. Directions also combine well, for example Thousand on the front page with Explorable’s highlights inside Reading the evidence.

    Once you choose

    1. I move the sections listed above to their new pages and apply the shortened wording in all five languages (English first for your review, then the translations).
    2. I rewrite site.css and the template structure for the chosen direction, keeping every URL.
    3. I build the scroll effects with as little JavaScript as possible (CSS scroll-driven animation where browsers support it), so every page still reads fully with JavaScript off or reduced motion on.
    4. I self-host subset fonts with Cyrillic and Chinese handled, within the 9-request, 300 KB budget, and check phones, keyboard focus and contrast.

    These demos cover only the English home page, load fonts from Google Fonts, and their links go nowhere. With reduced motion switched on, every demo shows its content without animation.
